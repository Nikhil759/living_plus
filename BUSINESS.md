# Aangan: Business notes (draft, to revisit)

Pricing is not final. These are working assumptions for the strategy doc.

## Market context
- Society apps charge roughly ₹3–15 per flat per month; NoBrokerHood offers a free tier, and MyGate funds its free tier with ads.
- The average Indian society is around 230 flats.

## Pricing sketch
**Societies**
- Free: feed, notices, marketplace, basic amenity booking
- Pro: about ₹8/flat/month: concierge, ops agent, events with ticketing, analytics
- Enterprise: custom (large townships, builder groups): custom workflows, dedicated engineer. A services line, not the core.

**Residents**
- Plus: ₹49–99/month: priority amenity slots, event perks, unlimited listings
- Landlord (later): ₹99–199/month per property

**Transactions**
- Commission on paid event tickets (about 10%)
- Food stall fees (split 50/50 with the society)
- Opt-in local-business listings (never in notifications)

## Society revenue share ("the app pays for itself")
- 20% of Plus subscription revenue goes back to the society.
- Credited to the society fund or against its bill, **never to committee members personally**, with a transparent monthly statement.
- Society plan becomes free once 25% of flats are on Plus.
- Check tax/GST treatment of society income with a CA.

## Per-society model (250 flats, per month, assumptions to validate)
| Source | Assumption | Aangan keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Society plan | 250 × ₹8 | ₹2,000 |
| Plus | 20% adoption × ₹99, minus 20% share | ₹3,960 |
| Paid events | 4 events × 30 tickets × ₹300 × 10% | ₹3,600 |
| Stalls | about ₹5,000/month, 50% share | ₹2,500 |
| Local listings | 5 × ₹1,000 | ₹5,000 |
| **Total** | | **≈ ₹17,000/month (≈ ₹2 L/year)** |

The society receives about ₹3,500/month and pays ₹2,000, so it comes out ahead.

## Key insights
- Plus adoption barely moves the total; events, stalls and local listings do. That's why sharing subscription revenue is cheap to give away.
- Riskiest assumption: how many paid events and stall sales a society really generates per month. Validate in user interviews.
- $10M (≈ ₹85 Cr) needs roughly 4,000–5,000 societies. MyGate took years to reach about 27,000. A realistic 12-month range is 300–800 societies (₹6–16 Cr); show honestly what must be true to hit 4,000.
