# Fire drill on Saturday, 17 October 2026

A society-wide fire drill will be held on **Saturday, 17 October 2026, at 11:00 AM**.

- When the alarm sounds, leave your flat by the staircase (not the lifts) and walk to the assembly point on the central lawn near the amphitheatre.
- The drill takes about 30 minutes. Lifts will be paused during the drill.
- Residents who need help with stairs should inform the facility desk by 15 October so a volunteer can assist.
