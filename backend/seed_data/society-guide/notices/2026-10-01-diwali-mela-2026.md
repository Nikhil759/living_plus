# Diwali Mela 2026

Our Diwali Mela is on **Saturday, 7 November 2026, from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM** on the central lawn and the amphitheatre. There will be food and craft stalls by residents, a kids' corner, music and a rangoli competition.

## Stalls for residents
- Apply in the Living+ app (Events, Diwali Mela, Apply for a stall).
- **Applications close on Friday, 23 October 2026, at 6:00 PM.**
- 30 stalls in total: 18 food stalls and 12 craft or other stalls.
- Stall fee: ₹1,500 for a food stall, ₹800 for a craft or other stall. Pay in the app after your application is approved.
- To keep variety, a maximum of 3 stalls of the same food type (for example chaat, momos, desserts) will be approved.
- Stall spots will be assigned by 28 October 2026.
- Food stalls must use induction or gas cylinders with a regulator in good condition; no open wood or coal fires.

## Firecrackers
Diwali is on Sunday, 8 November 2026. Firecrackers are allowed only in the designated area on the central lawn, between 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM, on Diwali day.

Questions: Farhan Ali (Events and Community), via the Living+ help desk under Feedback.
