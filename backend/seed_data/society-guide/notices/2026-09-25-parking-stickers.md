# New 2026 parking stickers

New parking stickers are being issued as decided at the AGM. **Old stickers will not be valid from 1 November 2026.**

- Collect your stickers from the facility desk, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, any day.
- Bring the vehicle's registration certificate (RC) and your flat's access card.
- Tenants also need their RWA tenant registration.
- No charge for the first two stickers per flat; additional stickers cost ₹100 each.
- Vehicles without a valid sticker after 1 November may be asked to use visitor parking.
