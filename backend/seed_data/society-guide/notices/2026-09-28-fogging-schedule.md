# Mosquito fogging schedule for October 2026

Fogging will be done every **Wednesday and Saturday in October, from 6:00 PM to 7:00 PM**, across all towers, basements and gardens.

- Please keep windows and balcony doors closed during fogging.
- Keep children and pets indoors during and for 30 minutes after fogging.
- Report stagnant water anywhere in the society through the Living+ help desk (category: Cleanliness).
