# Minutes of the Annual General Meeting, 24 August 2026

**Venue:** Community Hall, Prestige Meridian Park. **Time:** 11:00 AM to 1:40 PM.
**Attendance:** 186 of 420 flats represented (in person or by proxy). Quorum confirmed.
**Chaired by:** Anil Khanna, President.

## 1. Election of the Managing Committee, 2026–2028
Elected unopposed: Anil Khanna (President, Tower A), Meera Sharma (Secretary, Tower A), Vikram Rao (Treasurer, Tower D), Sunita Joshi (Member, Facilities, Tower B), Farhan Ali (Member, Events and Community, Tower C).

## 2. Accounts and maintenance charges
- The Treasurer presented the audited accounts for FY 2025–26. Approved unanimously.
- **Decision:** Maintenance increased from ₹4.30 to ₹4.50 per sq ft per month from 1 September 2026, mainly to cover higher electricity and security costs. Sinking fund contribution stays at ₹500 per flat per month.
- Late fee of 1.5% per month after the 15th retained.

## 3. Living+ community app
- **Decision:** Living+ adopted as the official community app from 1 September 2026. Notices, help desk requests, amenity bookings, event hosting and approvals move to the app. Paper notice boards will carry only emergency notices.

## 4. Quiet hours and conduct
- **Decision:** Quiet hours now start at 10:30 PM (previously 11:00 PM) and run until 7:00 AM.
- Community Hall and Amphitheatre events must end by 10:30 PM.

## 5. Parking and EV charging
- **Decision:** Visitor parking limited to 4 hours between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM; overnight needs a pass.
- **Decision:** 12 EV charging points to be installed in basement B1 by 30 September 2026, billed at ₹15 per unit.
- New 2026 parking stickers to be issued; old stickers invalid from 1 November 2026.

## 6. Amenities
- **Decision:** Pool guest fee of ₹100 per guest per visit introduced, with a maximum of 2 guests per flat per visit.
- **Decision:** Badminton and tennis courts move to booking through Living+, with a limit of 2 hours per resident per court per day.
- Kids' badminton coaching continues on Badminton 1, Tuesdays and Thursdays, 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
- Pool filtration upgrade approved; the pool will close for 3 days in October.

## 7. Pets
- **Decision:** Pet park next to Tower D approved, open from 1 October 2026 (6:00 AM to 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM).
- Pet clean-up fine set at ₹500.

## 8. Security
- **Decision:** CCTV upgrade (new cameras in basements and lift lobbies) approved at a cost of ₹6.5 lakh from the sinking fund.

## 9. Events
- **Decision:** Diwali Mela to be held on Saturday, 7 November 2026, on the central lawn and amphitheatre, with resident food and craft stalls applied for through Living+.
- Farhan Ali to lead the events team; volunteers to sign up in the app.
