# Prestige Meridian Park Resident Handbook

Prestige Meridian Park is a gated community of four residential towers (A, B, C and D) in Gurugram, Haryana, managed by the Prestige Meridian Park Residents' Welfare Association (RWA). This handbook sets out the rules every resident, tenant, guest and staff member must follow. It was last revised at the Annual General Meeting held on 24 August 2026 and applies from 1 September 2026.

If a rule here conflicts with a later notice from the Managing Committee, the later notice applies until the next handbook revision.

## 1. Who to contact

### 1.1 RWA office and facility desk
- The RWA office is on the ground floor of the clubhouse.
- Office hours: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Closed on Sundays and national holidays.
- The facility desk (maintenance, lift bookings, work permits) is next to the RWA office and is open daily from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- Intercom extensions: Security control room 100, Facility desk 200, Clubhouse reception 300, RWA office 400.
- Most requests can be raised in the Living+ app under Help desk, and that is the preferred way.

### 1.3 Emergency numbers
- Ambulance: 108. Fire: 101. Police: 112.
- Society security control room (24×7): intercom 100.
- A first-aid kit and an AED (defibrillator) are kept at the clubhouse reception and at the Gate 1 security cabin.

## 2. Maintenance charges and payments
- Monthly maintenance is ₹4.50 per square foot of super area, plus ₹500 per flat towards the sinking fund.
- Bills are issued on the 1st of each month and are due by the 10th.
- A late fee of 1.5% per month applies to any amount unpaid after the 15th.
- Pay through the Living+ app, by bank transfer to the RWA account, or by cheque at the RWA office. Cash is not accepted.
- Flats with dues outstanding for more than 90 days cannot book amenities or host events until the dues are cleared.
- Owners are responsible for maintenance even when the flat is rented out, unless the rental agreement says otherwise and the RWA has been informed in writing.

## 3. Moving in and moving out (shifting)
- Shifting of furniture and household goods is allowed only between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
- No shifting on Sundays or national holidays.
- The service lift must be booked at least 48 hours in advance through the facility desk or the Living+ help desk. Only one shifting is allowed per tower per half-day slot (9:00 AM to 1:30 PM or 1:30 PM to 6:00 PM).
- A refundable deposit of ₹2,000 is collected at the time of booking and refunded within 7 days if there is no damage to lifts, corridors or common areas.
- The facility team fits protective padding in the service lift before every shifting.
- Moving trucks and tempos must use Gate 2 (service gate) and may stay inside for a maximum of 4 hours.
- To move out, tenants need a gate pass. The gate pass is issued only after all dues are cleared and, for tenants, the owner has given a no-objection note to the RWA.
- New residents must register in the Living+ app within 7 days of moving in.

## 4. Tenants
- Owners must register tenants with the RWA within 7 days of the start of the tenancy.
- Required documents: a copy of the rental agreement, a copy of police verification, a photo ID for each adult tenant, and a no-objection note from the owner.
- Once registered, tenants have the same access to amenities, events and the Living+ app as owners.
- Tenants may not sublet a flat or a room without the owner's written permission and RWA registration of the new occupant.
- Brokers are not permitted to enter the society to show flats without the owner or tenant accompanying them.

## 5. Guests and visitors
- All visitors are approved through the gate system. Residents can pre-approve expected guests in advance.
- Guests staying more than 3 consecutive nights must be registered at the facility desk with a photo ID.
- Visitor cars may park only in the visitor parking area near Gate 1, for a maximum of 4 hours between 8:00 AM and 10:00 PM. Overnight visitor parking needs a pass from the facility desk.
- Guests may use amenities only when accompanied by the resident host and only as allowed in Section 11 (for example, the swimming pool has its own guest rules).
- Residents are responsible for the conduct of their guests and for any damage they cause.

## 6. Domestic staff, drivers and service providers
- All domestic staff (house help, cooks, nannies, drivers) must be registered at the facility desk with a photo ID and police verification before they start work.
- Registered staff receive an access card. Lost cards are replaced for ₹300.
- Staff entry and exit is through Gate 3, between 6:00 AM and 10:00 PM. Live-in staff must be declared at registration.
- Staff may use any lift.
- Car cleaners must be registered and may clean cars only between 6:00 AM and 10:00 AM.
- The RWA keeps a list of empanelled vendors (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, pest control, appliance repair) in the Living+ vendor directory.

## 7. Deliveries and couriers
- Food and grocery delivery staff may come up to the flat door between 7:00 AM and 11:00 PM if the resident approves the entry.
- Between 11:00 PM and 7:00 AM, deliveries are handed over at Gate 1.
- Couriers and parcels left at the gate are kept in the parcel room at the Gate 1 security cabin. Parcels must be collected within 3 days; after that they are returned to the courier.
- Large deliveries (furniture, appliances) follow the shifting rules in Section 3 if the service lift is needed.

## 8. Security and access
- Gate 1 (main gate) is open 24×7 for residents, visitors and deliveries.
- Gate 2 (service gate) is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM for moving trucks, contractors and bulk deliveries.
- Gate 3 (staff gate) is open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Each flat receives up to 4 resident access cards. Additional or replacement cards cost ₹300 each.
- CCTV covers all gates, lobbies, lifts, basements and common areas. Footage is kept for 30 days and is shared only with the police or with the committee's approval for a specific incident.
- Report suspicious activity to the security control room on intercom 100.

## 9. Parking
- Each 1 BHK and 2 BHK flat has one allotted covered parking slot; each 3 BHK and 4 BHK flat has two. Slots are allotted by flat and cannot be sold or rented to non-residents.
- Every resident vehicle must display a valid Prestige Meridian Park parking sticker. Stickers are issued by the facility desk.
- Do not park in fire lanes, driveways, ramps or another resident's slot. Wrongly parked vehicles may be wheel-clamped; release costs ₹500.
- Two-wheelers must be parked in the marked two-wheeler bays in basements B1 and B2.
- EV charging points are in basement B1. Charging costs ₹15 per unit and must be booked through the facility desk. Charging from a flat's own meter using extension cables is not allowed.
- Speed limit inside the society is 15 km/h. Children must not cycle or skate in the basements.

## 10. Amenities: general rules
- Amenities are for residents and registered tenants. Residents must carry their access card.
- Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult in all amenities unless a section below says otherwise.
- Bookable amenities (badminton courts and tennis court) are booked through the Living+ app. The community hall and amphitheatre are booked by hosting an event in the Living+ app.
- Proper sports footwear and clothing are mandatory.
- Food and drinks, except water in plastic or metal bottles, are not allowed inside the gym, courts or pool area.
- Amenities may close for maintenance. Closures are announced in the Living+ app at least 24 hours in advance, except in emergencies.
- Damage to equipment is charged to the resident responsible.

## 11. Amenity rules

### 11.1 Gym
- Timings: 5:30 AM to 10:00 PM daily.
- Minimum age 16. Residents aged 14 to 16 may use the gym only with a parent present.
- Wear clean indoor sports shoes. Outdoor shoes are not allowed.
- Wipe down equipment after use and return weights to the racks.
- When others are waiting, limit cardio machines (treadmills, cycles, cross-trainers) to 30 minutes.
- Personal trainers from outside must register at the facility desk and pay ₹1,000 per month.
- The gym is usually busiest from 6:00 AM to 8:00 AM and 7:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

### 11.2 Swimming pool
- Timings: 6:00 AM to 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM to 9:00 PM.
- The pool is closed every Monday for cleaning.
- A lifeguard is on duty whenever the pool is open. Follow the lifeguard's instructions at all times.
- Proper swimwear is mandatory. Shower before entering the pool.
- Children under 12 must be accompanied in the pool area by an adult. Children who cannot swim must use floats.
- Guests: a resident may bring a maximum of 2 guests per visit. Each guest pays ₹100 per visit at the pool desk, and the resident must stay with their guests throughout. Guest entries are recorded at the pool desk.
- No food, glass bottles or loud music in the pool area.
- People with open wounds, skin infections or contagious illness must not use the pool.

### 11.3 Badminton courts (Badminton 1 and Badminton 2, indoor)
- Timings: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.
- Booked in 1-hour slots through the Living+ app, up to 7 days ahead.
- Each resident may book a maximum of 2 hours per day per court.
- Non-marking shoes only.
- Kids' badminton coaching uses Badminton 1 every Tuesday and Thursday from 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM; the court cannot be booked during these hours.
- If you can't use your slot, cancel it in the app so someone else can book it. Residents who miss 3 bookings in a month without cancelling cannot book for the next 7 days.

### 11.4 Tennis court
- Timings: 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.
- The court is closed daily from 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM for cleaning.
- Booking rules are the same as for the badminton courts: 1-hour slots, up to 7 days ahead, maximum 2 hours per day.
- Tennis shoes only.

### 11.5 Café Lounge
- Timings: 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM daily.
- Open to residents and their accompanied guests. No booking needed.
- The lounge is usually busiest between 5:00 PM and 8:00 PM.
- Small private gatherings of more than 8 people need the facility desk's permission.

### 11.6 Clubhouse Terrace
- Timings: 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- Used for yoga, small meet-ups and community events of up to 20 people.
- Groups of 10 or more must host an event in the Living+ app.

### 11.7 Community Hall
- Capacity: 120 seated.
- Booked by hosting an event in the Living+ app. Every booking needs Managing Committee approval.
- Society-hosted and free community events: no charge.
- Private functions (birthdays, anniversaries, family gatherings): ₹3,000 per 4-hour slot plus a refundable deposit of ₹5,000.
- Events must end by 10:30 PM. Music must be kept at a low volume after 10:00 PM.
- The host is responsible for cleaning up and returning furniture to its place. Decorations must not damage walls (no nails, no adhesive tape on paint).
- Catering is allowed. Cooking with open flames inside the hall is not allowed; use the service kitchen.

### 11.8 Amphitheatre
- Capacity: 200.
- Available from 4:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
- Booking, charges and approvals are the same as for the Community Hall.
- Amplified sound must stop at 10:00 PM.

### 11.9 Children's play area and pet park
- Children's play area: 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Children under 10 must be supervised by an adult.
- Pets are not allowed in the children's play area.
- The pet park is next to Tower D and is open from 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM to 9:00 PM.

## 12. Events and community activities
- Residents can host free events, paid events and community gatherings through the Living+ app.
- Free events that don't use the community hall or amphitheatre are published immediately.
- Paid events, society-wide events and any event using the community hall or amphitheatre need Managing Committee approval before they are published.
- Hosting paid events is available to Living+ Plus members.
- Society-hosted festivals (such as the Diwali Mela) may offer food and craft stalls to residents. Stall applications open in the Living+ app with a deadline; the committee allocates stalls and avoids too many stalls of the same type.
- Commercial events by non-residents are not allowed.

## 13. Renovation and interior work
- Apply for a work permit at least 7 days before work starts, through the Living+ help desk or the facility desk.
- A refundable deposit of ₹10,000 is collected with the work permit and refunded after inspection when the work is complete.
- Working hours: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.
- Noisy work (drilling, breaking, cutting tiles or stone) is allowed only from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
- No renovation work of any kind on Sundays or national holidays.
- Contractors and workers must carry work passes issued by the facility desk and must use Gate 2.
- Debris must be packed in bags and removed by the contractor every day through the service lift. Dumping debris in common areas, shafts or the garbage chute is not allowed.
- Structural changes (removing walls, columns or beams) are not allowed without written approval from the Managing Committee and a certificate from a qualified structural engineer.
- Enclosing balconies or changing the external facade is not allowed.
- Air-conditioner outdoor units must be placed only in the designated ledges.

## 14. Pets
- Register every pet at the facility desk with a vaccination record. Vaccinations must be kept up to date.
- Dogs must be on a leash in all common areas, lifts and lobbies.
- Pets may use any lift. If another person in the lift is uncomfortable, please wait for the next one.
- Owners must clean up after their pets immediately. Failure to clean up attracts a fine of ₹500.
- Pets are not allowed in the swimming pool area, gym, clubhouse interiors, Café Lounge or children's play area.
- Persistent barking or nuisance complaints are handled by the committee after a written warning.

## 15. Noise and conduct
- Quiet hours are from 10:30 PM to 7:00 AM every day. During quiet hours, keep music, television and conversations in common areas at a low volume.
- Parties inside flats are allowed, but music must be at a low volume after 10:00 PM.
- Firecrackers are allowed only on Diwali, only in the designated area on the central lawn, and only between 7:00 PM and 10:00 PM.
- Smoking is not allowed in lifts, lobbies, corridors, basements, the clubhouse or any amenity. A designated smoking area is next to Gate 2.
- Clothes must not be dried on railings facing the front of the towers.
- Feeding of stray animals is allowed only at the designated feeding spot near Gate 3, between 7:00 AM and 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM and 8:00 PM.

## 16. Waste management
- Segregate waste into wet (green bin), dry (blue bin) and sanitary or hazardous (red bag).
- Doorstep garbage collection happens daily between 7:00 AM and 9:00 AM.
- Unsegregated waste will not be collected.
- E-waste (old electronics, batteries, bulbs) is collected on the first Saturday of every month at the clubhouse parking from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
- Bulky items (old furniture, mattresses) are collected on request; raise a request through the Living+ help desk.
- Garden waste from balconies goes in the green bin, in small amounts.

## 17. Water and power
- Water is supplied 24×7. Planned maintenance shutdowns are announced in the Living+ app at least 24 hours in advance.
- Power backup covers lifts, common areas and up to 3 kW per flat during outages.
- Report water leaks, low pressure or power problems through the Living+ help desk. Leaks and outages are treated as urgent.
- Do not tamper with common water lines, meters or electrical panels.

## 18. Safety and emergencies
- In a fire, use the staircases, not the lifts. Every floor has two fire exits and fire extinguishers near the lift lobby.
- The emergency assembly point is the central lawn near the amphitheatre.
- Fire drills are held twice a year; dates are announced in the Living+ app.
- Do not block staircases, fire exits or corridors with shoes, furniture or bicycles.
- Gas cylinders must be kept inside the kitchen. Piped gas leaks must be reported to the security control room immediately on intercom 100.

## 19. Complaints and the help desk
- Raise issues through the Help desk in the Living+ app. Choose the right category and mark it Urgent if there is a safety risk, someone is stuck in a lift, or there is no water or power.
- If the same common-area issue has already been reported, add yourself to it ("Me too") instead of creating a new one.
- Target response times: urgent issues within 2 hours; other issues within 48 hours.
- If an issue is not resolved within 5 working days, it is escalated to the Managing Committee automatically.
- You can give suggestions or feedback to the committee through Give feedback in the Help desk, anonymously if you prefer.

## 20. Marketplace, local businesses and flat openings
- The Living+ marketplace is for residents selling their own second-hand items. Commercial resellers are not allowed.
- Residents may list a small business or service they run themselves (for example tiffin, tuition, babysitting, pet care, handmade art). New business listings need a one-time approval from the Managing Committee.
- Businesses run from flats must not cause nuisance, attract a continuous flow of outsiders, or use common areas without permission.
- Flat openings may be posted only by owners or registered tenants of that flat. Brokers may not post listings.
- Never share another resident's flat number or phone number publicly.
